The loop

IRequest for quote

We turn a spec into a brief that suppliers can price, with quantity, target unit cost and due date.

IISupplier match

We find shops that fit the part, the volume and the region, then ask each one to quote in writing.

IIISamples

We order samples, track them to our desk and hold them until a QC check passes.

IVQuality control

We inspect against your drawing and tolerance, and record the result with photos and measurements.

VPurchase orders

We issue the order only after you approve the price, the terms and the budget bounds you set.

VILogistics

We book freight, follow the shipment and check the delivery against the order.

Who it is for

Teams that need parts, enclosures, cables or small production runs made to a spec, and checked before they ship. Hardware makers, product teams and makers who buy across several suppliers and want one place to run the work.

Silk Road Sourcing does not sell the parts. It sources them for you, under an order you approve.

What we do not claim

This site describes the service. It does not list live orders, completed jobs, client names or results. Ask for a brief and we will tell you what we can source, and what we cannot.
