How it works

One loop, six steps, one record

Every order follows the same path. Each step has an owner, a record and an approval point. You can see where an order is at any time.

  1. Brief

    You describe the part. We ask for the drawing, the quantity, the target unit cost and the date you need it. We reply with what we can source and what we need from you. Nothing is sent to a supplier yet.

  2. Quote

    We send the brief to matched suppliers. Each quote is recorded with its price, lead time and terms. You see the comparison before anything is ordered.

  3. Sample

    When you want one, we order a sample and track it. The sample has its own status. Notes say what matched the spec and what did not.

  4. Inspect

    We check the part against your tolerance and record each measurement. A failed check goes back to the supplier with the record attached.

  5. Order

    We issue the purchase order only after your approval of the price, the terms and the budget bounds. An order change is a new version of the order.

  6. Deliver

    We book the freight, follow the shipment and check what arrives against the order. Shortfalls are recorded and reported to you.

Where the agents work

Agents on CommandAGI draft briefs, search for suppliers, keep the records and check the arithmetic. An agent does not spend money on its own. It acts under a standing grant that you give, with the limits you set.

Where people work

Our sourcing team makes the calls, visits the shops and inspects the parts. A named lead owns each order. Local workers work under anonymized seats until their identity checks are complete.

Our rules
  • We state only what has happened. A quote is not an order. A shipment is not a delivery until it arrives.
  • Every purchase needs your approval. We never spend past the budget you set.
  • Every step is recorded before it is sent, with the name of the person or agent who sent it.
  • When a supplier does not answer, we say so. We do not guess at the answer.
