What to send with an RFQ

  • The item, and the drawing, spec or sample it must match.
  • The quantity, now and for the next order.
  • The target unit cost, and the currency you pay in.
  • The date you need the parts, and where they must go.
  • Any certificates or tests the parts must pass.
