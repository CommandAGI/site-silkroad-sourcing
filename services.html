Services

What we source, and how you get it

Each service is a step in one procurement loop. Hire the full loop, or only the step you need. Each step names what you get, and how the work runs through agents and our sourcing team on CommandAGI.

RFQ

Request for quote

  • A written brief: item, drawing or spec, quantity, target unit cost and due date.
  • Quotes from suppliers we choose, compared on one sheet.
  • A short list with the trade-offs in plain words.

On CommandAGI: an agent drafts the brief from your files, and a sourcing rep checks it before it goes out.

Priced on request.

MATCH

Supplier match

  • Suppliers matched by process, material, volume and region.
  • Capacity and lead-time notes from each shortlisted shop.
  • Written answers to your questions, kept with the match.

On CommandAGI: the supplier-match lead works with an agent that searches and records each shop it contacts.

Priced on request.

SAMPLES

Samples

  • Sample orders placed and tracked to our desk.
  • Sample notes: what matched the spec and what did not.
  • Held samples, so you can approve before a bulk order.

On CommandAGI: each sample is a record with its own status, so nobody guesses where it is.

Priced on request.

QC

Quality control

  • Inspection against your drawing and tolerance.
  • Measurements, photos and a pass or fail with reasons.
  • Re-inspection after a fix, with the first result kept.

On CommandAGI: a sourcing quality auditor records each check, and the result is attached to the order.

Priced on request.

PO

Purchase orders

  • A purchase order with price, terms and delivery dates written out.
  • Budget bounds that you set before we start.
  • Changes to an order shown as a new version, never silently.

On CommandAGI: an order is sent only after an explicit approval from you, under a grant you give.

Priced on request.

FREIGHT

Logistics

  • Freight quotes and bookings with the carrier we choose.
  • Tracking from the supplier's dock to your door.
  • Delivery check against the order, with any shortfall recorded.

On CommandAGI: tracking updates are recorded as they arrive. We never state a delivery that has not happened.

Priced on request.

AUDIT

Supplier audits

  • On-site or remote checks of a supplier's process and paperwork.
  • A written audit with the findings and the open items.
  • A re-audit plan for the items that need it.

On CommandAGI: our electronics sourcing advisor sets the audit scope, and the findings are kept in the supplier's file.

Priced on request.

Who does the work

Silk Road Sourcing runs an agency model. Agents on CommandAGI do the search, the drafts and the records. Local sourcing workers, seated under anonymized seats until identity checks are complete, do the calls, the visits and the inspections. A named lead is responsible for each order.

You never pay for time we did not record. Every service is priced on request, against a brief.
